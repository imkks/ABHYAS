# Abhyas Content Requirements

This document tracks the content files needed from the Product Owner (PO) for the Abhyas app pilot (Phase 1 & 2).

Please place the finalized assets in the `content/` folder with a corresponding entry in `content/manifest.json`.

## Required Assets
- `content/audio/om_chant.mp3` - Audio file for OM chanting.
- `content/audio/bhramari.mp3` - Audio instructions/tones for Bhramari.
- `content/audio/yoga_nidra_20min.mp3` - 20 minute Yoga Nidra guided session.
- `content/audio/yoga_nidra_30min.mp3` - 30 minute Yoga Nidra guided session.
- `content/audio/yoga_nidra_45min.mp3` - 45 minute Yoga Nidra guided session.
- `content/images/mudras/gyan.png` - Image showing Gyan mudra.
- `content/images/mudras/chin.png` - Image showing Chin mudra.
- `content/images/mudras/dhyana.png` - Image showing Dhyana mudra.
